Private by default

Your resume stays with you.

CV++ has no hosted backend, telemetry, analytics, credential form, or localhost bridge on this website. Credentials, sessions, resume content, backups, and ERP PDFs remain on your computer; the local app talks directly to IIT KGP ERP.

First launch warnings

Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may ask for a one-time confirmation. macOS releases are not Apple-notarized. Only use files from the official release page.
